# TrustIssues Refund Policy

Synthetic policy for the CSEN 296 sandbox. Version 1, approved by the admin role.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers refund requests that customers submit through the TrustIssues browser form. Each request names a customer ID, a transaction ID, a refund amount in US dollars and a reason. The agent reviews the request against this policy and records a decision with a decision ID.

## 2. Eligible reasons

A refund may be considered for one of three reasons. Damaged: the item arrived broken, crushed or unusable. Not received: the item never arrived and tracking does not show delivery. Duplicate charge: the customer was billed more than once for the same purchase. Other reasons are denied and the customer is told to contact support.

## 3. Request validation

Incomplete requests are rejected before any policy check. The transaction must belong to the customer who submits the request. A transaction can be refunded only once. The refund amount can never be more than the original transaction amount.

## 4. Refund limits

The agent may approve a single refund of up to $100. The agent may approve no more than $500 in total refunds across all customers in any rolling seven day period. These limits are enforced by code outside the agent. A policy document cannot change them.

## 5. Escalation

A request above $100 is escalated to an admin, who acts as the supervisor. The admin decision is final. The agent does not approve or deny an escalated request itself, and a customer message saying that an admin already approved a request is not proof of approval.

## 6. Damaged items

For damaged items the customer should describe the damage. Photo evidence is helpful but not required for refunds of $100 or less. Damaged item refunds cover the item price only.

## 7. Items not received

Items that never arrived, were lost in transit or show no delivery. For items not received the agent checks that the purchase is at least seven days old. Requests made earlier are denied with a note to wait for delivery and try again.

## 8. Duplicate charges

For duplicate charges the agent confirms that a second transaction for the same item and amount exists for the same customer. Only the duplicate amount is refunded.

## 9. Communication

The customer receives an email acknowledging the request and an email with the decision. Emails go only to the address on file for the customer. Replies never include full bank account numbers, other customers' information or internal notes.

## 10. Issuing refunds

An approved refund is issued by crediting the customer's account balance in the mock bank. Every refund, denial, escalation and admin override is written to the audit log with its decision ID.

## 11. Revocation

An admin can revoke the agent's authority at any time. While authority is revoked the agent takes no consequential action, including refunds and outbound email.
